## 1. Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, applications and digital information from unauthorized access, attacks, damage or theft.

### Importance of Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is important at three major levels:

- **Personal:** Protects personal information, accounts, passwords and devices.
- **Organizational:** Protects company data, business operations and digital infrastructure.
- **Government:** Protects national information, public services and critical infrastructure.

## 2. The CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a fundamental cybersecurity model consisting of three principles.

### Confidentiality
Ensures that information is accessible only to authorized individuals.

Example: Using passwords and access controls to protect private information.

### Integrity
Ensures that information remains accurate and is not modified without authorization.

Example: Preventing unauthorized changes to financial records.

### Availability
Ensures that systems and information remain accessible when authorized users need them.

Example: Maintaining reliable systems and backups.

## 3. The AAA Framework

AAA stands for:

- **Authentication:** Verifying the identity of a user.
- **Authorization:** Determining what an authenticated user is allowed to access or do.
- **Accounting/Auditing:** Recording and monitoring user activities within a system.

These principles help organizations manage access and monitor system usage.

## 4. Audit Logs

An audit log is a record of activities performed within a computer system or application.

Audit logs can help security professionals identify suspicious activities, investigate incidents and monitor user behaviour.

**Example: Banking activity**

A bank may record:
- Account login activities.
- Transaction history.
- Changes to account information.
- Dates and times of activities.

These records help with monitoring and investigations.

## 5. Threats, Vulnerabilities, Exploits and Targets

These are important cybersecurity terms.

### Threat
A potential danger that could cause harm to a system or its information.

### Vulnerability
A weakness in a system that could be exploited.

### Exploit
A method or technique used to take advantage of a vulnerability.

### Target
The system, network, organization or information that an attacker intends to compromise.

Understanding the differences between these terms is important when studying cybersecurity threats.

## 6. Categories of Attackers

Different categories of individuals may engage in cyberattacks for different reasons.

Examples discussed in class include:

- **Script Kiddies:** Individuals who use existing tools or scripts without necessarily understanding how they work.
- **Hacktivists:** Individuals or groups who carry out cyber activities to promote political, social or ideological causes.
